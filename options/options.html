    How it works

    Lulltab uses the browser’s native tab discard. The tab stays in the strip; its page unloads from memory. Click it — or Wake in the popup — and the browser brings it back. Chrome pages, the active tab, and anything you whitelist are left alone.

    Alt+Shift+S sleeps the current tab. Right-click a page for Sleep / Never sleep this site.
